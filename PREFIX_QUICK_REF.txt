🛡️ MODERATION COMMANDS (Type * in Discord)

*ban <userid> [reason]
    Example: *ban 1234567890123456789 Spamming
    Needs: Ban Members permission

*unban <userid>
    Example: *unban 1234567890123456789
    Needs: Ban Members permission

*mute <@user> [duration] [reason]
    Example: *mute @Username 30 Being disruptive
    Example: *mute @Username (permanent)
    Needs: Timeout Members permission
    Duration in MINUTES

*unmute <@user>
    Example: *unmute @Username
    Needs: Timeout Members permission

*serverinfo or *server
    Shows server details
    No special permissions needed

*help
    Shows this help menu
    No special permissions needed

📝 QUICK TIPS

• Ban/Unban: Use USER IDs (17-19 digits)
• Mute/Unmute: Use @MENTIONS
• Duration is in MINUTES for mute
• Leave duration empty = permanent mute
• AI chat works WITHOUT * prefix
