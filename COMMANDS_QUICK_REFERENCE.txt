🛡️ MODERATION COMMANDS (Type / in Discord)

/ban <userid> [reason]
    Example: /ban userid:1234567890123456789 reason:Spamming
    Needs: Ban Members permission

/unban <userid>
    Example: /unban userid:1234567890123456789
    Needs: Ban Members permission

/mute <user> [duration] [reason]
    Example: /mute user:@Spammer duration:30 reason:Timeout
    Needs: Timeout Members permission
    Duration in minutes (omit for permanent)

/unmute <user>
    Example: /unmute user:@Username
    Needs: Timeout Members permission

/serverinfo
    Shows server details
    No special permissions needed
